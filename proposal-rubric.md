- Proposal rubric

    The proposal is graded out of 20 points. Each row gets its full points, part of them, or zero.

    Criterion | Points | Full points | Loses points for
    ---|---|---|---
    Problem | 6 | The problem is clear. An agent makes a series of decisions and each decision changes what comes next. The proposal explains why a one-time prediction or a fixed rule would not be enough. | A vague problem. A prediction or classification task called RL. Actions that do not affect later states.
    Domain | 4 | The problem comes from a real field outside data science, and the proposal says who faces it and why it matters to them. | A toy problem with no real user. A problem from data science itself (such as tuning a model).
    Mentor agreement | 4 | The proposal gives the mentor's name and where they work. The mentor agrees in writing (an email is enough) to check the problem and the reward and to give feedback in weeks 10 and 14. | No written agreement. A mentor who does not work in the problem's field.
    Track | 3 | The track is named with a short reason for picking it. A Scientist team states a research question. An Engineer team says who will use the system, and an Innovator team says who has the problem and what they do today. | No track named. A reason that does not match the track.
    Team | 3 | The team has 3 or 4 students, and the proposal lists each member's name and ID. | Fewer than 3 or more than 4 students. Missing names or IDs.
